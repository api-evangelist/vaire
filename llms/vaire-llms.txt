# Vaire Computing

> Vaire Computing is a deep-tech semiconductor startup building reversible computing chips for near-zero energy computing. Its reversible logic gates recover and recycle the energy of computation instead of dissipating it as heat, aiming to decouple energy consumption from growth in compute. Co-founded by Rodolfo Rosini (CEO) and Dr Hannah Earley (CTO); backed by Seedcamp, 7percent Ventures, and Lifeline Ventures. Vaire publishes no public API, SDK, or developer portal today.
